# Licence transition and historical releases

From this revision forward, original software rights controlled by the project
publisher are offered under the proprietary terms in `LICENSE`.
The earlier public versions of this repository were issued as
`AGPL-3.0-only`. The copyright holder's new licensing choice cannot cancel
those earlier grants or remove rights to use and redistribute qualifying older
revisions under the AGPL conditions.

The AS 4100 engineering implementation cites the MIT-licensed `steel-as`
reference by joegattas (Copyright 2024). Its original MIT notice is retained in
`docs/steel-as-LICENSE.txt` and must remain wherever that licence requires it.

Legacy Python distribution/import identifiers are kept to prevent breakage in
existing host integrations. A module's JSON provenance must report its licence
and source revision accurately. No proprietary rights are claimed in licensed
Australian Standards publications or third-party materials.
